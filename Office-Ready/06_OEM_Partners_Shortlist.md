## 1️⃣ 7 kW Wall-Mount Home Charging Kit

**Procurement specification:** AC single-phase, ~7 kW (32A), Type 2 socket or tethered cable, OCPP 1.6J (2.0.1 preferred), IP65/66, smart-app + RFID, OEM white-label. Target FOB: **$150–450/unit**.

**Bangladesh-specific requirements:**
- 35–45 °C + 95% humidity tolerance (IP66 minimum, derated thermal design)
- Surge protection ≥ Class II
- Phase-loss / brown-out tolerant (DESCO/DPDC grid)
- CE + UKCA certification
- MOQ ≤ 50 units

## 2️⃣ Standard 3-Pin Universal Charging Cable (Mode 2 EVSE)

**Procurement specification:** Portable Mode 2 EVSE, 5–6 m cable, UK 3-pin plug (BS 1363), Type 2 vehicle inlet, 6A / 8A / 10A / 13A / 16A current selector, Type A RCD + 30 mA trip, IP67 control box. Required certifications: CE / UKCA / TÜV. OEM/white-label.

**Bangladesh-specific requirements:**
- BS 1363 (UK-style) plug — universal Bangladesh mains socket

## 3️⃣ Station-Type DC Fast Charging Setup (60 / 120 / 180 / 240 kW)

**Procurement specification:** Standalone pedestal DC fast charger, 60–240 kW CCS2 + optional Type 2 AC auxiliary, OCPP 1.6J + 2.0.1, Dynamic Load Balancing, TFT or LED status display, payment terminal integration (NFC / QR / card), modular power cabinet, IP55 minimum, surge protection Class I + II, grid soft-start (BERC / DPDC requirement), operating ambient 0–55 °C.

**Bangladesh-specific requirements:**
- Grid-friendly: soft-start + reactive power handling (BERC requirement)
- Tropicalised cooling: derated output above 40 °C ambient
- Low kWh meter accuracy drift; modular replacement PCBs (low total cost of ownership)
- Warranty RMA via Shenzhen ↔ Chittagong shipping lane (~14–21 day target)
- DLB: 3–4 chargers sharing a single 100–200 kW sanctioned grid connection

## 🎯 Phase Deployment Strategy

1. **Initiate vendor engagement immediately** — Joint Tech (Cat 1), Pheilix / BESEN (Cat 2), and Iocharger (Cat 3) as the three primary partners. Request formal quotations, product samples, and certification documentation.
2. **Request a parallel quotation from EVB / Beny** covering both Category 1 and Category 3. Compare the consolidation pricing against the two-vendor primary path before final commitment.
3. **Reserve Autel Energy for the premium segment only.** Do not lead with Autel on price-sensitive residential rollouts. Allocate to luxury hotels and premium residential estates.
4. **Engage StarCharge for Phase 2 / flagship DC hubs** (180 kW+ sites) after confirming their CharIN membership and Bangladesh / Southeast Asia regional coverage.
5. **Phase 1 (Jul–Dec 2026):** Joint Tech AC wallboxes + Pheilix portable cables + Iocharger DC stations — single freight corridor out of Xiamen and Shanghai, three purchase orders, three warranty pools.
6. **Phase 2 (Jan–Jun 2027):** Add EVB as a second-source DC supplier. Introduce StarCharge for 180 kW+ flagship highway and commercial sites. Evaluate TGOOD for any government or utility tender requirements.
7. **Commission 2–5 sample units per vendor** before placing any bulk purchase order. Indicative budget: $1,500–3,500 per vendor including inbound freight.
8. **Confirm OCPP 2.0.1 firmware** at sample-unit stage. Request a live CPMS integration demonstration — several vendors market 2.0.1 capability but ship with 1.6J firmware in production units.

## ⚠️ Key Risks

| Risk | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| **Import Duty — SRO Confirmation** | 0% duty waiver requires a Statutory Regulatory Order (SRO) number on the customs entry form (HS 8504.40.55.00). Request a sample commercial invoice and Bangladesh customs clearance history from each shortlisted supplier. |
| **BSTI Product Clearance** | BSTI review for EV charging equipment remains informal. Ask vendors whether they hold any prior BSTI acceptance correspondence for equivalent SKUs to reduce clearance risk at the port. |
| **Foreign Exchange Rate** | All prices use 1 USD ≈ 117 BDT (June 2026). Spot rates at time of purchase likely closer to 119 BDT/USD. All purchase contracts should use vendor-quoted rates at order confirmation. |
| **Sample Order Requirement** | 2–5 sample units per shortlisted vendor required before bulk commitment. Indicative cost: $1,500–3,500 per vendor including inbound freight. |
| **OCPP 2.0.1 Firmware** | Several vendors list OCPP 2.0.1 in marketing materials but ship 1.6J firmware in production units. Live CPMS integration demonstration and firmware version documentation must be required at sample stage. |
| **Pricing Basis** | All price indications are based on vendor-published FOB ranges and 2025–2026 trade-press surveys. Formal quotations are required before any procurement decision. |
