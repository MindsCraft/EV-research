🎯 Phase Deployment Strategy

  • 1
    Initiate vendor engagement immediately — Joint Tech (Cat 1), Pheilix / BESEN (Cat 2), and Iocharger (Cat 3) as the three primary partners. Request formal quotations, product samples, and certification documentation.
  • 2
    Request a parallel quotation from EVB / Beny covering both Category 1 and Category 3 together. Compare the consolidation pricing against the two-vendor primary path before final commitment.
  • 3
    Reserve Autel Energy for the premium segment only. Do not lead with Autel on price-sensitive residential rollouts. Allocate to luxury hotels and premium residential estates where brand and reliability command a margin premium.
  • 4
    Engage StarCharge for Phase 2 / flagship DC hubs (180 kW+ sites) after confirming their CharIN membership and Bangladesh / Southeast Asia regional coverage.
  • 5
    Phase 1 (Jul–Dec 2026): Joint Tech AC wallboxes + Pheilix portable cables + Iocharger DC stations — single freight corridor out of Xiamen and Shanghai, three purchase orders, three warranty pools.
  • 6
    Phase 2 (Jan–Jun 2027): Add EVB as a second-source DC supplier. Introduce StarCharge for 180 kW+ flagship highway and commercial sites. Evaluate TGOOD for any government or utility tender requirements.
  • 7
    Commission 2–5 sample units per vendor before placing any bulk purchase order. Budget approximately $1,500–3,500 per vendor for samples and inbound freight.
  • 8
    Confirm OCPP 2.0.1 firmware at sample-unit stage. Request a live CPMS integration demonstration — several vendors market 2.0.1 capability but ship with 1.6J firmware in production units.

⚠️ Key Risks

Import Duty — SRO Confirmation

The 0% duty waiver requires a Statutory Regulatory Order (SRO) number on the customs entry form (HS 8504.40.55.00). No vendor has yet supplied SRO-trackable documentation. Request a sample commercial invoice and Bangladesh customs clearance history from each shortlisted supplier before placing orders.

BSTI Product Clearance

Bangladesh Standards & Testing Institution (BSTI) review for EV charging equipment remains informal. Vendors should be asked whether they hold any prior BSTI acceptance correspondence for equivalent SKUs to reduce clearance risk at the port.

Foreign Exchange Rate

All price indications in this document use 1 USD ≈ 117 BDT (June 2026 reference). Spot rates at time of purchase are likely closer to 119 BDT/USD. All purchase contracts should use vendor-quoted rates at the time of order confirmation.

Sample Order Requirement

Board approval should include a provision for 2–5 sample units per shortlisted vendor prior to bulk commitment. Indicative cost: $1,500–3,500 per vendor including inbound freight. Sample testing is essential before any large-scale procurement.

OCPP 2.0.1 Firmware Discrepancy

Several vendors list OCPP 2.0.1 capability in marketing materials but currently ship 1.6J firmware in production units. A live CPMS integration demonstration and firmware version documentation must be required at the sample stage.

Pricing Basis

All price indications are based on vendor-published FOB ranges and 2025–2026 trade-press RFQ surveys. No vendor publishes binding FOB prices on their public website. Formal quotations are required before any procurement decision.
