⚡ Bottom Line — Recommended Decision

Two-vendor strategy: Joint Tech (AC volume) + Iocharger (DC fast + grid-constrained sites)

No single vendor wins on every criterion. Joint Tech gives us the best cost/certification balance for high-volume AC wallbox deployment across homes, hotels, and offices, and has explicitly built tropical-climate-rated hardware for our exact region. Iocharger gives us the strongest DC fast-charging technology — its Dynamic Load Balancing is the single most important feature for Bangladesh's weak grid infrastructure, letting us install multiple fast chargers on connections that can't otherwise support them. Autel Energy is our recommended backup/premium option for OEM-branded flagship sites, given its proven regional deployment (Bangkok, with Zeekr) and first-mover exclusivity opportunity in Bangladesh. We do not recommend a single-vendor exclusive contract at this stage — dual-sourcing protects us against factory delays and gives us pricing leverage.

Recommendation for the Board

Primary AC supplier: Joint Tech. Highest weighted score (86/100), lowest cost, and the only vendor actively engineering for our exact climate and region. Begin sample ordering (2–4 units) immediately and request their formal distributor agreement.

Primary DC / grid-constrained supplier: Iocharger. Second-highest score (84/100) and the clear technical leader on the single feature that matters most for Bangladesh — Dynamic Load Balancing. Their OCPP 2.0.1 certification also future-proofs our platform choice.

Secondary/flagship supplier: Autel Energy. Reserve for premium hotel and city-hub sites where OEM branding and proven regional deployment (Bangkok) matter more than unit cost. Pursue exclusivity discussion with their Dubai IMEA office now, before a competitor claims it — recall Rancon/Mercedes already moved fast on a similar exclusivity play with StarCharge-adjacent hardware.

Do not single-source. None of these vendors has a confirmed operating distributor relationship in Bangladesh today — meaning all of them are equally "first contact." Splitting our Phase 1 order across two vendors (Joint Tech + Iocharger) protects us from factory delays, customs issues, or quality problems with any single shipment, and gives us negotiating leverage on Phase 2 volume pricing.
